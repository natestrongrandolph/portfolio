Contract of Love

2025  ·  13 min  ·  Drama  ·  Producer

A Chinese father flies to America to visit his daughter — an art curator quietly fighting for her place — and reveals his true motive: a property deed meant to bind her future to his expectations. A story of love entangled with duty, pride, and silence.

Sole producer · Directed by Nebula Li · Graduate thesis film. Led a 30+ person international cast and crew on a bilingual English–Mandarin production, from development through post. Secured the Hilbert Museum as the hero location through direct negotiation — cutting the fee from $2,500 to $1,500 by packaging a promo-video trade — and delivered the $10,000+ production on schedule and under budget.
